DividedHearts

Founded 1995  ·  Resurfacing 2026

"Helping you, help others, help themselves."

DividedHearts began in 1995. No funding. No building. No authority. Just a screen, a purple notebook, and a woman who had been through enough to know that the people who actually save you are almost never the ones with the clipboards.

The community that grew here was sick, scattered, and broke — and some of the most capable people on the internet. They disagreed with each other. They argued. They drove each other half out of their minds. And they kept talking anyway, because the talking was the thing. None of us knew how to solve our own problems. But we had plenty of ideas about how others could solve theirs. So we helped each other. And the unsolvable got solved.

All of us hurting alone could have stayed that way. We didn't.

Falling Together

The story of a woman who built DividedHearts from nothing — medicated, unhoused, raising three children, and teaching herself to code by hand — and what that community made possible. A memoir about a woman who refused to go under — and the strangers, the degrees, the losses, and the love that shaped everything that followed.
